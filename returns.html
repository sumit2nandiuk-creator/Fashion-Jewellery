Returns & exchanges

Last updated 4 November 2026

We want you to love what arrives. If something is not right, you can return most pieces within 14 days of delivery.

What can be returned

  • Unworn pieces in their original packaging with tags intact, within 14 days of delivery.
  • Items that arrived damaged or different from what you ordered.

Not eligible: pierced earrings (for hygiene reasons), custom or made-to-order pieces, and items that have been worn or altered.

How to return

  • Message us on WhatsApp or email within 14 days with your order reference and the reason.
  • We will confirm the return address and the courier to use.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer, unless the item was faulty or incorrect.

Refunds

Once the piece reaches us and passes inspection, we refund within 7–10 working days to the original payment method. Refunds are made in Indian rupees; your bank or card issuer may apply its own exchange rate.

Exchanges

Sizing exchanges are free for bangles and rings within India. For international exchanges, the return and re-shipping costs are payable by the customer.
