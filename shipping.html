Shipping & delivery

Last updated 4 November 2026

Every order ships from our store in Baghajatin, Kolkata. Pieces are packed in a cotton pouch, then in a protective box, and sent with tracking and insurance.

Dispatch times

  • In stock: dispatched within 1–2 working days.
  • Made to order: 7–10 working days, as each piece is finished by hand.

Delivery estimates

  • India: 2–4 working days · free on orders over ₹999.
  • UK & Europe: 6–10 working days by tracked courier.
  • USA & Canada: 7–12 working days by tracked courier.
  • Rest of the world: 10–18 working days by tracked courier.

International shipping is quoted for your address before payment — message us on WhatsApp or email and we will share the exact rate.

Customs, duties and taxes

Orders are shipped from India. Import duties, VAT and handling fees charged by your country are payable by the recipient and are not included in the price paid to us.

Tracking

You will receive a tracking number by email or WhatsApp once the parcel leaves us. If tracking shows no movement for 5 working days, contact us and we will follow it up with the carrier.

Lost or damaged parcels

All shipments are insured. If a parcel arrives damaged, send us photographs of the outer packaging and the piece within 48 hours of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or refund.
