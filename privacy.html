Privacy policy

Last updated 4 November 2026

This policy explains what we collect when you browse or order from Fashion Jewellery, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Order details: your name, delivery address, email, phone number and the pieces you order — shared with us when you message on WhatsApp or email.
  • Payment and shipping records needed to fulfil the order and handle returns.
  • Shopping bag: the pieces you add are saved in your own browser (localStorage) on your device. This never reaches our servers and can be cleared by clearing your browser data.
  • Currency preference: also saved locally in your browser.

What we do not do

  • We do not run advertising or analytics trackers on this site.
  • We do not sell or rent your details.

Third parties

We share only what is needed with our courier partners and payment providers. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the font files are requested.

Keeping your data

Order records are kept for as long as required for tax and warranty purposes. You can ask us to delete details we no longer need.

Your rights

Write to us to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to ask how it is used. If you are in the UK or EU, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.
